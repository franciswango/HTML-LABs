How Borrowing Works

Follow these simple steps to borrow a book from our library:

  1. Register for a membership (free of charge).
  2. Visit the library and present your membership card at the front desk.
  3. Choose up to 5 books from any category.
  4. Books may be kept for 14 days and renewed once.
  5. Return books on time to avoid a small late-return fee.

Library Rules & Borrowing Policy

We ask all members and visitors to respect the following rules:

  • Maintain silence or speak quietly inside the library.
  • No food or drinks allowed near the bookshelves.
  • Handle all books with care — do not fold pages or write in borrowed books.
  • Return books by the due date stamped on your borrowing slip.
  • Lost books must be replaced or paid for at the current purchase price.
  • Children under 10 must be accompanied by an adult.

Late return fee: KES 10 per book per day after the due date.
