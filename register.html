Why Become a Member?

  • Borrow up to 5 books at a time for 14 days each.
  • Access to free Wi-Fi within the library premises.
  • Receive newsletters with new arrivals and library events.
  • Reserve books in advance from our catalogue.
  • Access to our children's reading programmes and workshops.
  • Use of private study rooms (booking required).

What Happens After You Register?

  1. Your application will be reviewed within 1–2 business days.
  2. You will receive a confirmation email at the address you provided.
  3. Visit the library with a valid ID to collect your membership card.
  4. You may begin borrowing books immediately upon receiving your card!

For any queries, return to our Home page for contact details, or explore our Book Collection while you wait.

Note for Children & Students Under 18

If registering a child under the age of 18, a parent or guardian must accompany the child to the library to co-sign the membership agreement. The child's membership will be linked to the guardian's account for borrowing supervision.
